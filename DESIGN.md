## Brand & Style

This design system targets ultra-high-net-worth individuals, premier property investors, and seekers of secluded, bespoke coastal sanctuaries. The ethos channels the timeless prestige of nautical heritage spliced with contemporary architectural refinement. Every touchpoint communicates tranquility, quiet confidence, permanence, and uncompromised craftsmanship.

The visual direction merges refined **Minimalism** with subtle **Tonal Glassmorphism** and maritime architectural framing. The experience avoids gaudy ornamentation in favor of expansive negative space, proportional precision, cinematic editorial layouts, and tactile, luminescent micro-interactions. Interface surfaces evoke the meeting point of deep oceanic depths, sunlight refracted through crystalline waters, and refined architectural metallics.

## Colors

The palette mirrors maritime topography and refined architectural finishes:
- **Primary Navy (`#0A1E2E`):** Represents deep abyssal marine trenches. Applied across key structural headers, navigational bars, authoritative display components, and primary containers.
- **Champagne Gold (`#D4AF37`):** The signature accent of luxury and sunlight on warm sands. Used with deliberate restraint on primary call-to-actions, refined hairline accents, active focus rings, and milestone badge treatments.
- **Coastal Slate Blue (`#336B87`):** The mid-tone oceanic hue serving as secondary interaction accents, pill badges, active tab markers, and subtle surface washes.
- **Foam Pearl White (`#F7FAFC`):** The primary canvas surface, delivering airy, sunlit breathing room reminiscent of bleached travertine and whitecaps.
- **Obsidian Navy (`#0D171E`):** High-contrast, high-legibility ink for all editorial body copy, captions, and structural borders, softening pure harsh black into an oceanic hue.
- **Subsurface Pearl Tint (`#EDF2F7`):** Neutral surface variant used for alternating editorial card blocks and inner component backing.

## Typography

The typography pairings orchestrate an editorial dynamic suited for private estate portfolios:
- **Playfair Display (Display & Headlines):** Offers high-contrast transitional serif elegance, lending estate titles and key property valuations an authoritative, architectural presence.
- **Montserrat (Titles & Body Copy):** Provides geometric modernist clarity and open counters. Body styles leverage light and regular weights with generous line heights to preserve visual calm.
- **Be Vietnam Pro (Labels, Overlines, Data Specs):** Delivers clean utilitarian precision for micro-copy, square footages, geographical coordinates, and pricing metadata. Labels leverage uppercase styling with expanded letter spacing to evoke maritime navigational instruments.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored on an asymmetrical 12-column responsive fluid grid with a maximum content canvas width of 1440px:
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, 1.5rem gutters, 4rem canvas margins. Content favors expansive wide-ratio layouts (7:5 split columns for visual assets versus narrative text).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, 1.25rem gutters, 2rem margins. Cards reflow into balanced 2-up grids.
- **Mobile (< 767px):** 4 columns, 1rem gutters, 1.25rem margins. Stacked single-column layouts with full-bleed imagery.

Vertical rhythm is intentionally expansive; sections rely on generous multiples of `space-xl` (ranging from 4.5rem to 7.5rem) to reinforce exclusivity, avoid clutter, and reflect coastal horizons.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids heavy drop shadows, relying on layered optical clarity and tonal luminosity:
- **Base Canvas:** Pearl White (`#F7FAFC`) provides the baseline zero elevation.
- **Level 1 (Property Panels & Inset Sections):** Tonal elevation using `#FFFFFF` with a sheer, micro-fine rim border (`1px solid rgba(13, 23, 30, 0.06)`).
- **Level 2 (Cards & Elevated Modals):** Diffused ocean-mist ambient shadow tinted with Deep Ocean Navy:
  `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(10, 30, 46, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(10, 30, 46, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Navigation Bars & Floating Overlays):** Architectural glassmorphism featuring `rgba(247, 250, 252, 0.82)` background, `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, accompanied by a top hairline accent of Champagne Gold (`#D4AF37`) at 30% opacity.
- **Focus & Interaction State:** Hovering interactive elements triggers a subtle golden bloom: `0 8px 24px -2px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.18)`.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a **Soft** shape language (`roundedness: 1`). 
- Default radius: `0.25rem` (4px) for interactive elements (buttons, form inputs, chips).
- Card and modal corners: `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / 8px).
- Architectural asset previews and image frames: `0.25rem` or crisp `0px` depending on editorial treatment, referencing stone lintels and clean seaside glass facades.
- Overline tags and status dots preserve minimal geometric forms without adopting overly playful or cartoonish circular radii.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background in Deep Ocean Navy (`#0A1E2E`) with Champagne Gold (`#D4AF37`) typography and subtle border (`1px solid #D4AF37`). Hover shifts surface to Coastal Slate (`#336B87`) with an amber ambient glow.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent surface with a hairline border (`1px solid #0A1E2E`), text in Obsidian Navy (`#0D171E`). On hover: background fills with `rgba(10, 30, 46, 0.04)`.
- **Gold Accent (High-Value Actions):** Metallic Champagne Gold fill (`#D4AF37`), deep obsidian navy text (`#0D171E`), font weight 600, uppercase letter-spaced metadata styling.

### Cards (Estates & Residences)
- Elevated `#FFFFFF` surfaces bounded by a hairline stroke (`rgba(10, 30, 46, 0.06)`).
- Visuals sit edge-to-edge across the top with a 16:10 aspect ratio.
- Property details use Playfair Display for pricing and residence titles, paired with a three-part grid metadata row for square footage, bedroom suites, and ocean frontage metrics in Be Vietnam Pro.

### Input Fields & Selects
- Background of Pearl White (`#F7FAFC`), bordered with subtle slate navy (`rgba(10, 30, 46, 0.15)`).
- Focus states transition border color smoothly to Champagne Gold (`#D4AF37`) with a 2px outer golden wash (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.20)`).
- Labels sit above the field in uppercase Be Vietnam Pro tracking +0.08em.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Tags (Exclusive, Reserved, Private Sale):** Slate Blue-tinted base (`rgba(51, 107, 135, 0.08)`), text in Coastal Slate (`#336B87`), 1px hairline border in `rgba(51, 107, 135, 0.20)`.
- **Heritage / Landmark Tags:** Deep Ocean Navy background (`#0A1E2E`) with Champagne Gold text (`#D4AF37`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Base control: 18px square (checkbox) or circle (radio), framed in 1.5px Coastal Slate (`#336B87`).
- Active checked state: Solid Deep Ocean Navy fill (`#0A1E2E`) featuring a crisp Champagne Gold checkmark or inner core dot.

### Specialized Architectural Components
- **Amenity Spec Strip:** Monospaced architectural metric grid with thin vertical rules separating waterfront acreage, yacht berth length, and private aviation proximity.
- **Interactive Horizon Carousel:** Unbounded cinematic image stage with minimal geometric navigation arrows and gold active slide progress lines.
